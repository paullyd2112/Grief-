## 1. Our commitments

What you share on Ndo is about some of the hardest moments of your life. We
treat it that way. This policy explains what [CONFIRM: legal entity name]
("Ndo," "we") collects, why, and what you control. In plain words:

- We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted
  advertising.
- We don't use AI in intake, matching, or moderation, and we don't use your
  information or conversations to train AI models.
- People, not algorithms, review matches and reports.
- You can delete your account at any time.

This policy is part of the Ndo Terms of Service.

## 2. What we collect

| Category | Examples | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account information | Name or display name, email, password, date of birth or age confirmation | You, at sign-up |

| Device and usage data | Device type, app version, IP address, crash logs, basic in-app activity | Collected automatically [CONFIRM: analytics tools used, if any] |

**Sensitive information.** Intake answers and messages about your loss, grief,
or mental health may count as sensitive personal information under some laws.
We collect it only to provide the Service, and we use it only for the purposes
in Section 3.

## 3. How we use it and who sees it

We use your information to:

- Create and run your account
- Match you with other members, based on your intake answers
- Deliver your messages to your matches
- Review reports, enforce the Code of Conduct, and keep members safe, including
  surfacing crisis resources when a message is flagged
- Respond to feedback and support requests and improve the app
- Handle billing once paid plans begin
- Meet legal obligations

Who can see it:

- **Your matches** see what you send them and the profile details you choose to
  share. [CONFIRM: which intake details, if any, are shown to a match.]
- **Ndo staff** may review intake answers to make matches, and may review
  messages only when they are reported or flagged, or when needed for safety,
  legal, or security reasons. [CONFIRM: review practice matches this.]
- **Service providers** who help us run Ndo (for example hosting, email, and
  payment processing) process data on our behalf under contracts that limit
  their use of it. They may not sell it or use it for their own purposes.
  [CONFIRM: list of providers.]
- **Legal and safety.** We may disclose information if required by law, or if
  we believe in good faith it's needed to prevent serious harm to someone.
- **Business transfers.** If Ndo is sold or merged, your information may
  transfer to the new owner, who must honor this policy or notify you of
  changes.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral
advertising, or use it to train AI models.

## 4. Retention, deletion, and security

**Retention.** We keep your information while your account is active and only
as long as needed for the purposes above.

**Deleting your account.** You can delete your account in the app. When you do,
we delete or de-identify your account and intake information within [CONFIRM:
e.g., 30 days]. Messages you already sent may remain visible to the person you
sent them to, [CONFIRM: or are removed from both sides]. We may keep limited
records longer where needed for safety or legal reasons, such as records of a
ban to prevent a removed member from rejoining, or where the law requires it.

**Security.** We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, such
as encryption in transit and access limited to staff who need it, to protect
your information. [CONFIRM: encryption at rest and other measures in place.] No
system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. If a
breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you as required by law.

## 5. Your rights and choices

Everyone can:

- Update your profile and intake answers in the app
- Choose what you share with a match
- End matches and block members
- Delete your account at any time
- Opt out of non-essential emails using the unsubscribe link

Depending on where you live (including California and other US states with
privacy laws), you may also have the right to:

- Know what personal information we collect about you and how we use it
- Get a copy of your personal information
- Correct information that's wrong
- Delete your personal information
- Limit our use of sensitive personal information (we already use it only to
  provide the Service)
- Not be treated differently for using any of these rights

To make a request, contact us (Section 6). We'll verify your identity before
acting on it and respond within the time the law requires. [CONFIRM with

## 6. Age limit, changes, and contact

**Adults only.** Ndo is only for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect
information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we'll delete the account
and its information.

**Changes to this policy.** If we make material changes, we'll notify you in the
app or by email before they take effect, and update the date at the top.
